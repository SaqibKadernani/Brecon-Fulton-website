02  |  Low-Energy & AC-Free Comfort Testing

Passive & Active AC-Free Cooling Simulations

Conventional air conditioning is expensive to install and run in hot climates. We simulate passive and active AC-free cooling strategies before construction begins, testing how shade structures, natural airflow, earth-air ventilation, thermal curtains, and low-energy fans keep spaces comfortable without traditional AC units.

Typical Applications

Outdoor shaded walkways and cycle tracks, semi-enclosed public spaces, low-energy residential developments, and warehouses seeking AC-free cooling.

What We Deliver
  • /Airflow and shade canopy simulations to block peak summer heat
  • /Passive cooling comparisons using earth-air tunnels and thermal curtains
  • /Active AC-free ventilation sizing using low-energy exhaust and circulation fans
  • /Clear reports comparing comfort temperatures, capital cost, and energy savings

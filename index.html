02  |  Low-Energy & AC-Free Comfort Testing

Passive & Active AC-Free Cooling Simulations

Conventional air conditioning is expensive to install and run in hot climates. We simulate passive and active AC-free cooling strategies before construction begins, testing how shade structures, natural airflow, earth-air ventilation, thermal curtains, and low-energy fans keep spaces comfortable without traditional AC units.

Typical Applications

Outdoor shaded walkways and cycle tracks, semi-enclosed public spaces, low-energy residential developments, and warehouses seeking AC-free cooling.

What We Deliver
  • /Airflow and shade canopy simulations to block peak summer heat
  • /Passive cooling comparisons using earth-air tunnels and thermal curtains
  • /Active AC-free ventilation sizing using low-energy exhaust and circulation fans
  • /Clear reports comparing comfort temperatures, capital cost, and energy savings

04  /  Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Brecon & Fulton Consulting do?

Brecon & Fulton Consulting is an independent engineering and AI software consultancy based in Dubai. It builds AI workflow and task management tools, simulates passive and active AC-free cooling strategies, evaluates construction robotics and autonomous equipment, advises on modular and low-income housing systems, and supports industrial localization and automated manufacturing projects.

Who does the firm work with?

The firm works with property developers, industrial operators and technology ventures, mainly in the UAE, and has delivered projects in the UK as well. Client and partner names on past projects are kept confidential.

What is an AC-free cooling simulation?

It is a simulation, run before construction begins, that tests how shade structures, natural airflow, earth-air ventilation, thermal curtains and low-energy fans keep a space comfortable without conventional air conditioning. The resulting report compares comfort temperatures, capital cost and energy savings.

Can you evaluate construction robotics suppliers independently?

Yes. The firm provides independent evaluations of construction robotics and autonomous platforms before capital is committed. This covers building design compatibility, comparison of equipment manufacturers and distributors, site logistics and summer heat planning, and written decision records for leadership and regulators.

Do you support industrial localization and new manufacturing projects?

Yes. The firm helps industrial groups and investors decide which products to manufacture in the UAE, benchmarks them against imported supply, identifies licensing and joint-venture partners, and develops automated factory concepts and investment cases.

What kind of AI workflow tools do you build?

Custom AI task and workflow tools for teams that want less admin and clearer daily priorities than traditional project trackers give them. Typical features include AI task boards, automated routing and deadline reminders, conversion of meeting notes and emails into assigned tasks, and executive dashboards that show bottlenecks and priorities.

Who leads the firm?

Saqib Kadernani is Managing Director and Principal Consultant. He holds a BEng in Mechanical Engineering from Swansea University and an MSc in Biomedical Engineering with Distinction from Queen Mary University of London.
